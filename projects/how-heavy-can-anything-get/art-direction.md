# Art direction: "How Heavy Can Anything Get?" (video 18, the Weight Column)

- **Idea:** video 17's log ladder pointing UP in kilograms. One rail, one heavy bronze-white marker climbing rung by rung from
  YOU (~70 kg) to SGR A*; the ZEROS counter (the power of ten) is the second readout. The rail breaks at the top for the
  observable universe, then an honest LINEAR bar comparison shows dark matter ~5 : 1.
- **Background:** ink #0B0E16 (top) -> #05060A (bottom), faint dust + 2 % grain. No camera; the rail never moves.
- **Colour:** steel rail and chips, bone text; the marker #FFD9A8 (also the black hole's photon ring); amber ONLY on the
  ordinary-matter bar; violet ONLY on the dark-matter bar and its labels.
- **Type:** Saira Condensed 700 (rung names 28 px), IBM Plex Mono (numbers 26 px, powers 28 px, labels >= 24 px), Bricolage
  Grotesque 800 captions, Spline Sans Mono tab. Powers of ten drawn raised by <Sci>.
- **Window (x 580-920):** S1 whale -> S2 Eiffel Tower (AI impression), S3 neutron star, then a DRAWN black hole inside a dashed
  Mercury's orbit (horizon ~1/5 of the orbit), 'drawn · not to scale'. From sc10 the column holds the matter bars ('drawn').
- **Motion (4):** eased climbs ending ON the seats, gravity-seated chips, accelerating counter runs, a still settle.
